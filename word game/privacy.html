Privacy Policy

Reword · Last updated 17 September 2026

Reword is a word game made by Rounded Corner, Inc. This policy explains what the game stores, why, and what you can ask us to do about it. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use any analytics or tracking services.

What Reword stores

  • A display name. Whatever you type in as your name, so opponents can tell whose turn it is. It does not have to be your real name.
  • An account identifier. A random id created when you first open the game. It is how your games find you.
  • Your games. Boards, racks, scores, moves and chat-free game history for the games you are in, so a game can continue across devices and sessions.
  • A notification token, if you allow notifications. It identifies your device to Apple's or the browser's push service so we can tell you it is your turn. It is stored where other players cannot read it, and it is deleted when you turn notifications off.

If you create an account

You can play without ever giving us an email address; by default your account is anonymous. If you choose to sign in with an email address and password, or with Sign in with Apple, that sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service. Your email address is held there so you can sign back in and reset your password. It is not attached to your games or shown to other players.

Who can see what

  • Other players see your display name and the games you share with them. Nothing else.
  • Google Firebase stores the game data and sign-in details on our behalf. See the Firebase privacy documentation.
  • Apple and browser push services deliver notifications. They receive the device token and the notification text, which says whose turn it is.
  • Vercel hosts the web version and keeps standard server logs.

We do not share your information with anyone else, and there are no advertising or analytics services in the game.

Children

Reword is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Keeping and deleting your data

Game data is kept while you are playing and for as long as your account exists.

You can delete your account from inside the game: open your account settings and choose Delete account. You will be asked to sign in again to confirm it is you. This permanently removes your player record, your notification token and your solo games.

Games you played against other people are shared with those players and are not deleted with your account; your display name remains on them so the game still makes sense to your opponent. If you want those removed as well, email cdm92@yahoo.com and we will take care of it.

Deleting the app removes the game from your device but does not by itself delete data already stored.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and stored with Google Firebase. Access rules restrict each player to their own games, and notification tokens cannot be read by other players. No system is perfectly secure, but we do not store payment details or contact information beyond a sign-in email address.
