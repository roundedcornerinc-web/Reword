Welcome to the next level of wordplay

Reword starts like a familiar word game but after the first move, things get interesting. Tiles on the board can be swapped, stolen, and reused, giving you more opportunities to put up big numbers.

Player Rack

No more luck of the draw

In other games, missing out on the premium tiles can leave you watching the big points go by. Not in Reword. Swap the N in your rack for the Z in CRAZE and put that 10-point beauty to work for your own move. Because sometimes you have to make your own luck.

Player Rack

Swap vowels for consonants and vice versa.

Stuck with a rack full of vowels? Drowning in consonants? In Reword, you may have a way forward without reaching for the tile bag. Swap the O in your rack for the W in MOWN, or the R in your rack for the A in PEAK, and you’re back in business without losing a turn.

Player Rack

The art of the steal

Reword lets you steal a letter from the beginning or end of a word on the board—as long as the word you leave behind is still valid. Take the S from PLAYS or the D from GAMED and put them to work in your play.

No hoarding allowed

You can swap or steal up to four letters each turn, but no saving them for a rainy play. Swapped and stolen tiles turn purple in your rack so you know they’re on loan and need to go back on the board in your move.

Keeping you in the game until the last letter

Reword’s unique gameplay means more strategy, more chaos, and far fewer games where one player gets ahead and spends the next 20 minutes polishing their victory speech. Leads can evaporate, tables can turn, and that smug letter wrangler on the other end of the screen may end up eating their... well... words.
